# Voice Recording Consent Form (Prototype)

**Project:** Voice Authentication for Wallet Payments — AI/data-science prototype
**Purpose:** research and development of a speaker verification / anti-spoofing
prototype. Not a production system; no payment integration.

By signing below, I agree to the following:

1. **Recording.** My voice will be recorded on a laptop microphone, speaking
   digit sequences and free-speech samples, across at least two sessions on
   different days and under varying noise/distance conditions.
2. **Cloning for attack tests.** A subset of my recordings may be used to
   generate synthetic (text-to-speech or voice-conversion) copies of my voice,
   for the sole purpose of testing whether the prototype's anti-spoofing
   component can detect such attacks against my own enrolled voice.
3. **Storage.** My recordings and any synthetic copies derived from them will
   be stored on the researcher's local machine and/or a private, access-
   controlled cloud storage bucket. They will not be shared publicly or with
   third parties, and will not be used to train any model outside this
   project without asking me again.
4. **Deletion.** All recordings and derived synthetic audio will be deleted
   by: **______________** (deletion date, no later than project end). I may
   request earlier deletion at any time by contacting the researcher.
5. **Withdrawal.** I may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal will not
   affect analyses already published in the project report, but my raw audio
   will be deleted upon request.
